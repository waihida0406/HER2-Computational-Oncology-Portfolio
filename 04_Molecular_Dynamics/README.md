# Project 4: Molecular Dynamics Simulation of HER2–Ligand Complex

## Status
🟡 In Progress

## Objective
To evaluate the stability of the HER2–ligand complex through molecular dynamics simulation.

## Software Used
- CHARMM-GUI
- GROMACS
- VMD

## Simulation Details
- Simulation Time: 100 ns
- Force Field: CHARMM36
- Water Model: TIP3P

## Workflow
1. System preparation.
2. Energy minimization.
3. NVT equilibration.
4. NPT equilibration.
5. Production MD simulation.
6. Trajectory analysis.

## Expected Results
- RMSD
- RMSF
- Radius of Gyration
- Hydrogen Bonds
- PCA
- DCCM
- MMGBSA
